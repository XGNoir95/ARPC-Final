# Website appearance

Click the sun/moon button once to toggle between **Light** and **Dark**. There is no selection menu. It stays above the scroll-to-top arrow in one fixed vertical stack on every page, including account and admin screens. Both controls share the same circular shape and dimensions: 44 pixels on phones and 60 pixels from the medium breakpoint. The arrow uses the brand green, remains visible at all times and is disabled when the page is already at the top. It enables as soon as the page is scrolled away from the top. The controls stay outside document flow and do not change navigation, form or content geometry.

The original light appearance is the default. Explicit choices are saved under `arpc-color-theme` in browser storage and synchronized between tabs. Storage restrictions do not prevent switching themes for the current visit. A small head script applies a saved dark choice before React mounts.

The palette lives in [index.css](../Frontend/src/index.css). White and pale content surfaces become neutral black and gray, with readable neutral foregrounds. In dark mode, green UI surfaces use approximately a 20% black tint of their existing colors: brand buttons, navigation, footer, team panels, catalogue illustrations and the course laptop frame. Light-mode colors and gold accents stay unchanged. Labels on darkened backgrounds keep readable contrast. Dark overrides are applied to the document, so dialogs rendered in portals use the same theme. Photo heroes, the account photo panel and the three About cards receive a subtle 20% black overlay in dark mode; their text and controls stay above it. The homepage fade follows the next section's surface color. Other photography, logos and cover artwork retain their original appearance; logo trays deliberately keep a white background. The official Google control uses its supported dark variant.

Theme tests cover direct toggling, saved preferences, cross-tab updates, blocked storage, first-paint initialization and Google rendering. Contrast tests verify the new dark surfaces and normal text against a 4.5:1 minimum, alongside the existing light content surfaces. The existing full frontend suite, lint, build and production style verification also apply.

Implementation follows the [Tailwind dark-mode documentation](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/dark-mode). Browser visual inspection is unavailable in this tool session.
